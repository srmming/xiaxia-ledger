Why Xiaxia Ledger

Logging isn't a chore. Reading isn't a headache.

What it does best is show you: open it and you know how this month is going. Leave the logging to the AI you already use.

  1. Everything on one screen

    How much is left this month, how fast you're spending, where it goes, whether your net worth grew. One point per page, no scrolling.

  2. Say it and it's logged

    “Lunch 12,50”, “import this bank statement”: just tell Claude, Codex or another AI, and it logs and categorises for you.

  3. Xiaxia gives you advice

    Where to put spare money, which subscription quietly got pricier, who still owes you: Xiaxia watches your ledger and reminds you.

  4. Your ledger stays yours

    The ledger is your own file on your Mac (with iCloud Drive turned on, it lives in your iCloud). It isn't uploaded to our servers and needs no bank password. Every change is recorded and can be undone.

Install

Three steps, one minute.

Requires macOS 14 Sonoma or later. To check your version, open the Apple menu in the top-left corner → About This Mac.

Download and drag to Applications

Click the download button above, open the downloaded xiaxia-ledger.dmg, and drag the 夏夏账本 icon onto the Applications folder next to it.

Open it the first time

In Applications, double-click 夏夏账本. Your Mac asks whether to open an app downloaded from the internet; click Open. From then on it behaves like any other app.

Connect your AI

On first launch a “Connect your AI” window appears. Click Connect next to your AI, restart the AI tool when asked, then try telling it “Log this: coffee 3,20”.

It won't open and says the developer can't be verified, or that it's damaged. What now?
  1. Close that dialog first.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll to the bottom.
  3. Where it says 夏夏账本 was blocked, click Open Anyway next to it and enter your Mac password.

Still stuck? Delete the dmg and download it again (an incomplete download causes this too).

The “Connect your AI” window didn't appear, or I'd rather connect later.

You can reopen the guide any time in the app under Settings → AI connections. Or copy the setup prompt and paste it to any AI; it will walk you through it.

I don't have any AI tool installed yet.

We suggest starting with Claude Desktop (it has a free tier). DeepSeek users can install Hermes Agent; Qwen or Kimi users can install Cherry Studio. Without any AI you can still add entries by hand in the app.

Where is my ledger stored? Is it safe?

The ledger lives on your own Mac (or in iCloud Drive if you use it). We run no server and cannot see your books. Note: when you let an AI read the ledger, those entries go to that AI provider, just like any chat with it. Xiaxia Ledger never touches bank passwords; statements are files you export and hand over yourself.

How do I update?

The app tells you when a new version is out; click Install Update and your ledger is untouched. You can also download it again here and install over the old one.
